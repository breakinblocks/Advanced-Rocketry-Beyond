# Armor sealing

The Chemical Reactor applies **Airtight Seal** to ordinary armor using a Pipe Sealer and titanium-aluminide sheets. Chest armor also needs an iridium pressure tank.

Existing armor components are preserved. Wear a full sealed set. Sealed chest armor stores a configurable oxygen buffer and refills on an Oxygen Charger. These recipes require the server's oxygen mechanics to be enabled.
