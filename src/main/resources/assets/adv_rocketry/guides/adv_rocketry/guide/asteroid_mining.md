# Asteroid mining missions

Select an asteroid at the Observatory and research its chip. Build an **uncrewed rocket** with a Mining Drill, the programmed chip and cargo storage.

Scan, assemble, fuel and launch. The rocket runs a timed mission and returns with cargo. Yield and duration depend on the target and settings. Launched missions retain their selected asteroid definition across configuration changes.

Unload returning cargo and verify chip and fuel before relaunch.
