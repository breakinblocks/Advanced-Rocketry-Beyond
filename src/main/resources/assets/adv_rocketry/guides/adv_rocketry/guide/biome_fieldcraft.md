# Biome fieldcraft

Bring back living specimens as well as ores. Riverbanks supply reeds and silt; caves shelter luminous plants and mineral blooms. A cave can have a different biome from the surface above it.

## Harvest and replant

Small plants drop themselves without special tools. Replant them on suitable support: soil for floor plants, a ceiling for hanging roots and seed clusters, and a wall for shelf fungi. Floating leaves need water below; aquatic fronds need water and a supported bed.

Rootwood and alien trees drop saplings from their leaves. Use shears or Silk Touch to collect the leaves themselves. Logs craft into four planks or yield six in a [Cutting Machine](cutting_machine.md). Use an axe to strip rootwood logs.

The ten planetary stone varieties work in ordinary stone tool and stone crafting recipes. Mine them with a pickaxe. Soils are quickest to harvest with a shovel.

## Cultivate a patch

Apply bone meal to a planted specimen to grow **one nearby offspring**. Leave empty space beside it. No valid space means no bone meal is consumed. Small plants do not spread automatically.

| Plant group | Support for new growth |
| --- | --- |
| Ferns, seedlings, groundcover, foliage, reeds, sedges and cave blossoms | Suitable soil, moss or mud |
| Hanging roots and luminous seed clusters | Supported ceiling positions |
| Shelf fungi | The same wall plane |
| Floating leaves | Water below |
| Aquatic fronds | A water source over a supported bed |
| Vent growth, ember curls, storm blades and spark mushrooms | Magma, cooling crust or weathered basalt |
| Mineral blooms | Mineral crust, calcite or amethyst blocks |
| Salt shrubs | Sand or alluvial silt |

These are the default supports. Servers can customize cultivation materials for heat plants, mineral blooms and salt shrubs; sturdy support is still required.

Heat and mineral plants do not compost. Other small plants, tree leaves and saplings can go in a composter.

## Process your harvest

These are the default yields; server recipes may differ. Each batch uses one plant species.

| Input | Method | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 jointed reeds | Hand crafting | 1 string |
| 3 jointed reeds, marsh sedge or hanging roots | Cutting Machine | 2 string |
| 3 jointed reeds or marsh sedge | Craft in a horizontal row | 3 paper |
| 4 luminous seed clusters, cave blossoms or spark mushrooms | Centrifuge | 1 glowstone dust |
| 4 mineral blooms | Centrifuge | 1 quartz |
| 4 alluvial silt | Centrifuge | 4 clay balls |
| 1 strike glass | Furnace | 1 glass |

All small plant species also have dye recipes. See the [Centrifuge](centrifuge.md) and [Cutting Machine](cutting_machine.md) for active processing recipes.

## Read the landscape

Look for short lines of fallen foliage along water edges and fungi on fallen logs. Nearby young growth changes with moisture: reeds and sedges beside wet rootwood, ferns on drier ground, and leaf litter around fallen alien wood.

Where habitat deposits generate, exposed alluvial silt can contain clay pockets. Mineral crust in mineral caves can contain calcite; volcanic cooling crust can contain magma. Stormland strike glass can contain fused glass, which needs **Silk Touch** to collect. Deposit availability depends on the planet's generation settings.

Servers can extend these deposits to suitable biomes from other mods or disable individual deposit types.
