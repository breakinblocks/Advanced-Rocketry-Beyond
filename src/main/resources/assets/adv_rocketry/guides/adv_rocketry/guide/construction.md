# Construction and ports

Sneak-use the **Holographic Projector** to choose a structure and inspect its materials. Use it on a controller to preview that build, or on another block to position the selected preview. Sneak-scroll through layers.

Controllers face outward. Guide examples face north; use the projector for rotated builds. Keep required air spaces empty. The scenes show unformed construction blocks so every part remains visible before the modeled shell appears.

- **Input and Output Hatches:** supply ingredients and remove products.
- **Fluid Input and Output Hatches:** supply and collect fluids. Different fluids may need separate hatches.
- **Power Input Plug:** receives FE for processing. Power Output Plugs serve generators.
- **Machine Structure:** fills permitted casing positions. Advanced structure, blast brick and specialized parts are not freely interchangeable.

Open the controller to inspect formation and work status. Its menu exposes attached ports. Connect automation to the actual hatches and plugs.

Flexible positions need enough service ports for the intended recipe. Keep output capacity available, even for chance-based products. [Motors and coils](machine_upgrades.md) improve speed; [cables](cabling.md) automate supply.
