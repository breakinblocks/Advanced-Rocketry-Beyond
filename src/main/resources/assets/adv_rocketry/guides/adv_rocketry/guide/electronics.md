# Circuits and precision parts

Grow silicon boules and cut wafers. The Precision Laser Etcher prepares circuit plates; the Precision Assembler combines them with other parts.

Basic, tracking and advanced circuits serve different equipment. Item and fluid I/O boards are distinct components. Keep reusable lenses in machine inputs when requested.

Solar cells feed power equipment and saw blades feed cutting assemblies. Check each product below before starting a batch.
