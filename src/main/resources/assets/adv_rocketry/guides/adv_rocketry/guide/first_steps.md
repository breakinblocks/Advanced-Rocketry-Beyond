# Your first spaceflight

1. Mine iron, copper, redstone, tin and aluminum. Build a [Plate Press](plate_press.md) for early plates.
2. Make a [Coal Generator](coal_generator.md), power input plugs and hatches. Use the [projector](construction.md) to build a [Lathe](lathe.md), [Rolling Machine](rolling_machine.md) and [Arc Furnace](electric_arc_furnace.md).
3. Grow and cut silicon, then etch and assemble [circuits](electronics.md). These unlock rocket equipment and [space suits](suits.md).
4. Build an [Electrolyzer](electrolyzer.md) and [Chemical Reactor](chemical_reactor.md). Reserve oxygen for breathing and prepare the [propellant](propellants.md) your engines accept.
5. Build the [pad and assembler](rocket_building.md). Include engines, matching tanks, guidance and a seat. Scan and resolve the reported errors before assembly.
6. Charge your suit and check all armor slots. Pack spare oxygen and return fuel. Select Luna, fuel the rocket, board and launch.
7. After landing, crouch-use the rocket with an empty hand to open its controls. Check its return destination and fuel before exploring.

Luna has no breathable outdoor air. Bring [airless lighting](lighting.md) and keep your suit on until a sealed room is working. The Apollo landing site is near **X 2347, Z 67**.

If a step stalls, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
