# Fluid storage and pumps

Liquid Tanks hold **16,000 mB per block** and share fluid in vertical stacks. Breaking and replacing a tank preserves its contents.

The powered Fluid Pump drains connected source blocks into a 16-bucket buffer and exports to adjacent handlers. Supply FE and somewhere for the fluid to go. Use [fluid pipes](cabling.md) for longer routes and separate or lock networks for different fluids.

Oxygen supports life support and oxidizer systems. Hydrogen supports fuels and jetpacks. Nitrogen and oxygen feed terraforming. Gas giants provide other gases through [collection missions](gas_mining.md). Rocket fuel comes from processing; enriched lava feeds the centrifuge.

One bucket holds 1,000 mB. Check the actual amounts on the [Electrolyzer](electrolyzer.md), [Chemical Reactor](chemical_reactor.md) and [Centrifuge](centrifuge.md) pages.
