# Gas collection missions

At a station orbiting a gas giant, build an uncrewed rocket with Gas Intakes and fluid cargo hatches. Packed intakes contribute their collection rating.

Use the Atmosphere Analyzer on the rocket to cycle the giant's gases. Launch with the intended gas selected. The timed mission fills the hatches and returns without propellant use for the gas mission.

The Deployable Rocket Assembler scans a horizontal tower frame and launches intake rockets sideways from the station. Resolve its scan errors before assembly.

Unload into dedicated tanks or locked networks before changing gas selection.
