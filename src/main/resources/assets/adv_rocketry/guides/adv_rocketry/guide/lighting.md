# Lighting and barriers

Thermite Torches and Circle Lights work in airless environments. Ordinary torches extinguish where combustion is impossible and can be relit in a combustible atmosphere. The server controls extinguished-torch drops.

A Force Field Projector extends a barrier in its facing direction under redstone power, one block every five ticks. Removing the signal retracts it. Leave room for the path.

Test habitat sealing with a Seal Detector rather than relying only on a barrier's appearance.
