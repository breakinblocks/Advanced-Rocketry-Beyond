# Motors and coils

Advanced, enhanced and elite motors provide **1.5×, 2× and 4×** speed factors in compatible motor positions. Gold, aluminum, titanium and iridium coils can also shorten processing times in coil positions.

Upgrades do not replace arbitrary casing or power plugs. Both copper-coil variants are accepted where the projector permits copper coils. Faster machines need ingredients and output space more often. Recipe panels show base duration; the machine's progress reflects the actual build.
