# Planet surfaces and resources

Planets differ in temperature, atmosphere, gravity and biomes. Check air before leaving the rocket. Ores, craters, volcanoes and geodes depend on world definitions and settings.

Luna supplies turf and basalt. Crystal chasms and ocean spires have characteristic materials. Alien forests supply Lightwood: saplings grow tall branched trees and their planks glow. Stormland grows charred trunks and electric mushrooms.

Geodes enclose ore-lined cavities inside a hard shell. Craters may contain deposits in bowls, rims and central peaks. Save dilithium for warp travel.

Tin, aluminum, titanium and dilithium generate under default Overworld settings; vanilla supplies copper. Iridium ore generation is **disabled by default**, so check processing and orbital sources instead of assuming surface deposits.

Pack oxygen, return fuel and suitable tools. Use [beacons](beacon.md) to mark important sites.
