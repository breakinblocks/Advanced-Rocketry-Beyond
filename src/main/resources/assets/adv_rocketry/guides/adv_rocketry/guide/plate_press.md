# Plate Press

Place **obsidian**, the recipe's **metal block directly above it**, and the **Plate Press directly above the metal block**. The press points down.

Apply a redstone pulse. The press consumes the block and drops plates below itself. Remove power before the next operation. It needs no FE. Collect the drops or provide nearby collection equipment.

The [Rolling Machine](rolling_machine.md) offers powered plate production.
