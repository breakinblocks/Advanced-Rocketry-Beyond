# Rocket cargo and control

Loaders and unloaders exchange cargo with packed inventories and tanks in a stationary rocket. Include compatible cargo hatches in the rocket and connect the appropriate local conduits or pipes to its ground equipment.

The Guidance Access Hatch edits the packed guidance computer. A programmed planet chip changes the destination without disassembly.

Use a Linker on infrastructure and then the rocket or assembler. Sneak-use linked infrastructure to unlink it. A Monitoring Station reports fuel and flight status and launches the rocket on a **redstone rising edge**.

A Satellite Hatch carries built satellites and packed station modules for deployment. Retain the payload's programmed chip. Let loaders finish before pulsing the launch signal.
