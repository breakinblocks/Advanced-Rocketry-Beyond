# Build and assemble a rocket

Build a contiguous launch-pad footprint and a structure tower beside it. The tower sets the scan height and must reach above the rocket. Place and power the Rocket Builder beside the pad.

Build engines, matching tanks and guidance inside the scan frame. Add a seat for a crewed rocket and cargo or satellite hatches as needed. Decorative blocks add mass; rescan after design changes.

Use **Scan** and inspect mass, thrust, capacity and errors. Once valid, use **Build**. Assembly time depends on frame volume, consumes **100 FE/t**, pauses without power and rescans before moving blocks.

Use the assembled rocket with an empty hand to board its seat. Crouch-use it for controls; a seatless rocket opens controls on ordinary use. The menu offers inventory, destination, station pad, launch and disassembly.

Fuel and choose a destination before launch. A linked assembler or monitor can launch uncrewed rockets. Continue with [propellants](propellants.md), [guidance](guidance.md) and [cargo automation](rocket_automation.md).
