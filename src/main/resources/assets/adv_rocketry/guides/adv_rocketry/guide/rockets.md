# Rockets and travel

Build on a launch pad, scan, assemble, fuel and choose a destination. A crewed flight also needs a seat and life support.

Use a pickaxe to recover placed rocket motors, tanks, builders, guidance computers, launch pads, seats and mining drills. Breaking these components by hand does not return the block.
