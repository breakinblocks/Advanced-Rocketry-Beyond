# Space suits and oxygen

Wear a complete suit before entering an airless environment. The chestpiece carries oxygen in its installed pressure tanks; a helmet alone is not sufficient.

Put armor in the Suit Workstation to manage modules. Install compatible chest tanks and fill them with oxygen. Stand on an Oxygen Charger supplied with oxygen to refill compatible tanks. The charger also supplies hydrogen for jetpack tanks.

Check the suit HUD before departure and pack oxygen for exploration and the return trip. Keep a spare supply even when planning to build a habitat.

See [Suit upgrades](suit_upgrades.md) or [seal ordinary armor](armor_sealing.md) in the Chemical Reactor.
