# Troubleshooting

## A machine does not form

Use the projector one layer at a time. Check facing, specialist casing, required air gaps, and input versus output ports. Flexible positions still have a limited list of alternatives.

## A formed machine stays idle

Check power, exact ingredients and fluids, reusable tools, and all outputs. Chance-based products still need space. Reopen recipe pages after a server reload.

## A cable transfers nothing

Set the source face to Extract. Check redstone, filters, fluid locks and destination capacity. Disabled faces disconnect branches. The route must be loaded. One energy output remains limited to 5,000 FE/t.

## A rocket will not launch

Read its status. Check thrust against mass, compatible propellant and tanks, destination and fuel requirement. Finish assembly. Mining and gas missions need their specific uncrewed designs.

## A room has no breathable air

Keep your suit on. Check oxygen, FE, doors, gaps, pipe-sealer frames and room-size limits. Use the Seal Detector and Atmosphere Analyzer.

## A station or satellite service fails

Check identifiers, planetary system, power and required orbit. Elevators need safe altitude and orientation; warp cannot proceed while anchored.

## Recipes differ from defaults

Servers can change costs, world generation, atmosphere and recipes with datapacks. Live panels show the active collection. Ask the operator when progression differs from the standard route.
